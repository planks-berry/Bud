# Modelled device specification

The device modelled by Bud is the Sonicware deconstruct MINIMAL, an 11-track groovebox
released May 2026.

> **Provenance of this document.** Everything below was reconstructed from published
> manufacturer specifications and press coverage. Entries marked **[P]** are *provisional* —
> inferred rather than stated, and to be corrected against the official user manual
> (`docs/reference/MINIMAL_manual_en.pdf`) before the interface is built. When the manual
> contradicts a value here, fix it *here first*, then propagate through
> `source/core/params/ParameterIds.h`, which is the single source of truth for the code.

Public sources state "9 drum tracks" (1–9), a loop track (10) and a bass synth (11), with
tracks 7–9 doubling as one-shot sampler tracks. The specific voice occupying tracks 2 and 4–6
is not stated anywhere public; the assignment above is a working default.

## Sound set

16 drum kits, 10 banks, ~130 sounds. Bud ships an original synthesized equivalent generated by
`tools/factory_gen` (see [Provenance](../README.md#provenance)) plus full user sample import.

## Sampler

48 kHz / 16-bit linear PCM.

| Bank | Slots | Length | Channels | Used by | Tempo handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2 | 32 | 2 s | mono | tracks 7–9 | Repitch to tempo |
| S4 | 16 | 4 s | mono | tracks 7–9 | Repitch to tempo |
| S8 | 12 | 8 s | stereo | track 10 | Time stretch |

Track 10 additionally has switchable loop/one-shot playback, a crossfade setting, and
retrigger. Sampling sources are LINE IN and USB.

## Bass synth (track 11)

Monophonic, inspired by a classic 303-style bass synthesizer.

- Waveforms: `SAW`, `SQUARE`, `TRIANGLE`, `RECTANGLE`
- Sub oscillator: sine, range `-2`, `-1`, `UNISON`; can bypass filter and sends
- Filter: 4-pole low-pass with resonance
- Envelope: decay with adjustable decay curve
- Glide (slide): adjustable time *and* curve, entered per step
- Gate time: adjustable
- Accent: linked to the filter
- Overdrive: dedicated, built in
- `S01` mode: continuous blend between SAW and SQUARE, with a rectangle-wave sub oscillator

## Sequencer

- 16 steps × 4 variations (A/B/C/D) per pattern; chaining A→B→C→D gives up to 64 steps
- 128 patterns
- Per-track step length 1–16
- Per-track note length 1/1 to 1/32 (the step time division — this is what makes tracks
  polymetric against each other) **[P]** *interpretation to confirm*
- Real-time and step recording
- Parameter locks — knob movements recorded per step
- Sub-steps (retriggers within a step)
- Swing, accent / de-accent, random velocity
- Phrase rotation
- Variation copy and paste, free A/B/C/D chaining, insert-fill playback
- Mute functions and solo mode

## FEEL — drift models

The signature feature. Three profiles — `808`, `909`, `MINIMAL` — each applying subtle
micro-timing and pitch drift **per voice rather than as a global swing**, so a hi-hat can drift
on one curve while the kick stays locked. Modelled as a seeded, deterministic modulation source
per voice; exact curves are proprietary and are tuned by ear against the hardware.

## Effects

**Master** (performance-oriented, one macro knob each): Sweep Filter, Phaser, Distortion,
Snip Loop (captures a slice of live playback to stutter / repeat / freeze), Ducking Compressor.

**Sends:** Reverb ×3 — Room, Hall, Plate. Tape-echo style delay with BPM sync and ping-pong.

## Per-track control knobs

| Knob | Function |
|---|---|
| A | EQ / filter frequency |
| B | EQ / filter resonance or gain |
| C | Effect send |
| D | Level |

## Panel

27 pads/keys, 12 control knobs, 2 encoders, 16 micro knobs, character display.
Control modes: `SCALE`, `LATCH`, `JUMP`.
Physical: 297 × 176 × 48 mm, 840 g.

## I/O

LINE IN (stereo mini), LINE OUT (stereo mini), headphone out (stereo mini),
MIDI/SYNC IN and MIDI/SYNC OUT (3.5 mm), USB-C (data, MIDI, audio; audio interface can be
disabled), built-in speaker. Powered by DC 9 V or 6× AA. Clock master or slave.
