For grown-ups

Stage

How much the toys put out. This sets every toy at once; below, each can be moved on by itself. Nothing is ever locked.

The toys are at different stages. Choosing one here sets them all.

The toys

Which are on the shelf, where, and the stage of each. The list runs in the shelf's order, left to right and top row first; the arrows move a toy earlier or later. Fewer choices help a young child settle into one: put a few out and rotate them every week or two. Move a toy on when your child starts pushing at what it can do.

New things

A new game arrives on the shelf above, marked new, with its own switch. Each new part of the playroom has a switch here. All start on; turn off anything you would rather keep back for now.

Over five minutes the sun in the window sets and the moon rises; then the toys go to sleep, the next time the shelf is out. In a game, the house button shows the moon once it is up.

Setting up the iPad

  1. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The playroom then opens full screen, and once opened it works without a connection.
  2. Turn on Guided Access (Settings → General → Accessibility → Guided Access). Triple-click the Home button inside the playroom to keep the iPad in it.
  3. Turn off multitasking Gestures (Settings → General → Multitasking & Dock), so a whole hand on the glass cannot leave the app.

This playroom needs Safari on iOS 11 or later.
